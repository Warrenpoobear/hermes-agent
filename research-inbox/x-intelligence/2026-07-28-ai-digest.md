## Claim
A formal 21-page paper (arXiv:2607.21735) proves that a passing AI red-team evaluation demonstrates only that evaluators did not find dangerous capability under the conditions tested — not that dangerous capability is absent. The paper maps what categories of claims red-team evaluations can and cannot support, with reproducibility code and data.

Directly applicable to Hermes agent governance and Reliability Gate v1.0 (PR #19). The paper formalizes the gap exposed by the Jul 23 OpenAI/HF sandbox-escape incident: evaluation design must treat the agent as an untrusted process searching for task completion by any route, not a cooperative test-taker. Upgrades the theoretical basis for Hermes write-path sandboxing.
