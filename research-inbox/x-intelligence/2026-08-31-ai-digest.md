## Claim
Paper "Same Model, Different Harness: Different Coding-Agent Results" (arXiv 2608.26218, posted ~2026-08-28, surfaced via a bare-title rohanpaul_ai repost 2026-08-31 00:32 UTC) reports that a fixed harness-level "treatment" applied to the same model, under a tight-context/time-pressure configuration (169-task SWE-bench Verified cohort, 20,480-token window, 480-second attempt endpoint), raises mean per-task fail-to-pass fraction from 28% to 49% and complete solutions from 43 to 72 -- and the same frozen treatment, without model-specific retuning, raises both endpoints on three additional models of different designs. In wide-window comparisons the effect narrows.

## Why it matters
Directly evidences that coding-agent solve rate is harness-dependent, not just model-dependent -- and the effect appears to transfer across models without per-model tuning. This bears on Darren's #1 named infrastructure blocker: CI on Warrenpoobear/biotech-screener has been RED ~114+ days with PRs #576-#579 unmerged (lint, pytest 3.12, replay failures), the confirmed root cause of the Herald/Bellringer outages. If a comparable harness-level adjustment (context budgeting, stop conditions) improves fix-attempt completion on Hermes's own coding-agent fleet, it could accelerate CI remediation without touching model choice or governance boundaries.
