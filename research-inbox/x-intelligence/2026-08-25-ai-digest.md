## Claim
ASI-Bench (arXiv:2608.17271), a new benchmark of 60 project-level research tasks across 11 scientific domains built by 40+ experts, finds that across 18 frontier agent-model configurations the average score drops from 50.91 (full methodological guidance) to 29.10 (method specified only) to 26.62 (agent must select its own method) — current agentic systems remain heavily dependent on human methodological guidance for autonomous scientific research.

## Why it matters
Directly informs how much autonomy to grant Hermes/biotech-screener agents on open-ended research tasks (scientific literature synthesis, catalyst interpretation). It's hard evidence for keeping human-specified guardrails on research methodology rather than assuming agents can independently design and execute research plans — consistent with Darren's "restore infrastructure trust first" / deterministic-behavior operating discipline.

## Underlying source
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17271 ; open leaderboard: https://asibench.apexin.ai/ ; GitHub: https://github.com/apexin-ai/ASI-Bench

## Verification

## Potential workflow impact

## Smallest shadow test

## Final disposition
