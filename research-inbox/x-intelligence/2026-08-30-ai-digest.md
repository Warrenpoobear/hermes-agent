## Claim
Google Research's WikiSkill paper (arXiv 2608.27454) introduces a framework where AI agent skills co-evolve with a persistent, structured knowledge base (a "wiki"): raw execution experience, accumulated knowledge, and executable skills are kept in three separate layers (Raw / Wiki / Skills), with experience continuously consolidated into the wiki so future skill updates can build on it. Across diverse benchmarks and models, WikiSkill consistently outperforms state-of-the-art skill-evolution methods; skill evolution complements model scaling — smaller models with evolved skills can outperform substantially larger models without them. The paper builds explicitly on Andrej Karpathy's "LLM Wiki" concept of compiling agent experience into persistent, cumulative knowledge. Paper posted ~2026-08-27, surfaced via rohanpaul_ai repost 2026-08-29.

## Why it matters
This is an independent, benchmarked academic validation of the same architectural thesis behind Wake Robin's own PKOS (Personal Knowledge Operating System): separating raw experience from consolidated knowledge from actionable skills, with the knowledge layer as the thing that actually drives improvement (not the skill library itself).
