## Claim
Replit built and deployed an internal "truth layer" — a version-controlled, human-reviewed, model-independent knowledge corpus stored in a code repo rather than a document or database. Agents navigate it like a codebase (file trees, grep, diffs, blame) before answering, producing consistent grounded answers across teams.

## Why it matters
This is a concrete production deployment of organizational knowledge governance for multi-agent systems — not a research paper. The framing ("truth in a repo, not a doc") maps directly to the Town-Hermes memory sync problem: Hermes agents currently lack a single authoritative corpus of Wake Robin facts; each skill/session reinvents definitions. The pattern is also directly relevant to PKOS Stage 1 (pinned snapshot handoff) — the snapshot IS the truth layer for the biotech-screener stack.
